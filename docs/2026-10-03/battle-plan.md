## The recommendation

Take **Corsair from 75 to 99 first**. You already own the hardest shortcut—the first 75 levels—and COR gives you a useful party identity through Phantom Roll while Trusts carry much of the leveling workload. It is not the cheapest job to perfect, but it can become useful before its damage sets are finished.

Do not level BRD first. Modern leveling is not especially slow for BRD with Trusts, but serious BRD gearing can become a large project. Get the account moving on COR, establish i119, then choose BRD with clear eyes.

## The route

1. **Reorient:** unlock Trusts, Records of Eminence, modern travel, and the first Rhapsodies bonus. Start with [Return Setup](return-setup.md).
2. **Reach 99:** clear each cap quest just before it blocks you, keep RoE objectives active, and level with Trusts. Use [COR 75–99](cor-75-99.md).
3. **Replace the museum gear:** at 99, item level matters more than character level. Follow [After 99](post-99.md) from i117 to a coherent i119 set.
4. **Become group-useful:** build the small roll toolkit and habits in [Weekend-ready COR](weekend-cor.md).
5. **Choose for fun:** once the foundation works, use [Choose the Next Job](choose-next-job.md) rather than spreading effort across the whole roster.

## Three rules that save time

- **Advance Rhapsodies while leveling.** Its key items add cumulative experience bonuses, more Trust slots, and other quality-of-life benefits.
- **Upgrade by checkpoint, not by slot obsession.** Old gear is temporary; buy functional level-appropriate pieces and save the real optimization for i119.
- **Do not grind Job Points in level-75 gear.** Establish the i117/i119 floor first, then work on JP. Master Levels come much later.
