# Phase 2 — The Post-99 Queue

Level 99 is the start of modern character progression, not the finish. Follow this order so each activity makes the next one easier.

## 1. Establish the item-level floor

- [ ] <span data-task-id="sparks-117" data-task-phase="post-99" data-task-required="true"></span> **Equip a complete i117 baseline.** Use the level-99 Records of Eminence/Sparks options to replace old armor and weapons. Item level on applicable equipment also affects Trust strength, so this is an account-power upgrade, not cosmetic cleanup.

Buy functional coverage first:

- an i117 main-hand weapon for solo play;
- an i117 gun/ranged option and current ammunition;
- a full wearable armor baseline;
- basic accessories that support accuracy, ranged accuracy, and survivability.

Do not spend hours optimizing i117. Its job is to let you enter the content that replaces it.

## 2. Follow the tutorial bridge

- [ ] <span data-task-id="roe-intermediate" data-task-phase="post-99" data-task-required="true"></span> **Complete the tutorial and intermediate RoE objectives.** Work through the item-level progression prompts, unlock required battlefields/content, and keep the objective rewards. If an objective name differs from a guide, trust the current in-game RoE menu.

## 3. Start monthly repeatable gearing

- [ ] <span data-task-id="ambuscade-started" data-task-phase="post-99" data-task-required="true"></span> **Unlock and begin Ambuscade.** Start at a difficulty your Trust party can clear reliably. Monthly enemies, mechanics, and rewards rotate, so read the current primer before entering.

Priority for early currency:

1. A coherent armor set your COR can wear.
2. Upgrade materials that advance that set instead of scattering currency.
3. Useful capes and weapons only after the armor path is understood.

## 4. Build one coherent i119 set

- [ ] <span data-task-id="first-119-set" data-task-phase="post-99" data-task-required="true"></span> **Replace the baseline with a coherent i119 set.** “Coherent” means the pieces support the same job mode; it does not mean best in slot.

Maintain three small equipment intentions rather than one overloaded set:

- **Idle/survival:** damage reduction, movement, refresh where relevant.
- **Accuracy/damage:** enough accuracy for the content, then attack/ranged stats.
- **Roll support:** Phantom Roll potency/range/duration pieces as they become available.

## 5. Only then start vertical grind systems

- Obtain Job Breaker and begin Capacity Points after the i119 floor is real.
- Build Job Points alongside useful content rather than treating 2,100 JP as the admission price for all groups.
- Master Levels require a mastered job. They are a long-term layer, not the first post-99 todo.

Continue with [Weekend-ready COR](weekend-cor.md) when the i119 baseline is usable.
