# Phase 3 — Weekend-ready COR

The goal is not “perfect COR.” It is to be honest, prepared, and useful in approachable group content while the deeper sets grow.

## Minimum roll kit

- [ ] <span data-task-id="cor-roll-kit" data-task-phase="weekend" data-task-required="true"></span> **Build the minimum roll kit.** Own the dice your group commonly requests, keep COR-specific roll-enhancing pieces you obtain, and make equipment sets that do not require manual swapping mid-fight.

Common categories to cover:

| Party need | Rolls to understand |
| --- | --- |
| Physical damage | Chaos, Samurai, Fighter's, Hunter's |
| Magic damage | Wizard's, Warlock's |
| Sustain/support | Evoker's, Tactician's |
| Progression | Corsair's Roll when Capacity Points are the stated goal |

The leader may request a different pair. Ask before the pull. **Barataria Ring** and **Luzaf's Ring** are recognizable early utility targets; verify their current acquisition and use cases before buying or questing. Pursue roll-duration and job-specific pieces as the opportunities open. Premium weapons and multiple specialized damage sets are later projects.

## Practice the actual support loop

- [ ] <span data-task-id="cor-support-practice" data-task-phase="weekend" data-task-required="true"></span> **Practice on solo content:** apply the requested two rolls to the correct recipients, maintain them without letting both expire, avoid dangerous bust chasing, use Random Deal/Wild Card deliberately, and contribute damage only when positioning and mechanics allow.

Make macros or equipment-set commands for:

- each commonly requested roll;
- your roll-enhancing set;
- a safe idle/damage-reduction set;
- ranged/melee damage only after those basics work.

## Unlock job progression

- [ ] <span data-task-id="job-breaker" data-task-phase="weekend" data-task-required="true"></span> **Obtain Job Breaker and begin Job Points.** Capacity Points come from appropriate level-96+ enemies and are not earned in Abyssea. Mix JP with content that also advances gear or familiarity.
- [ ] <span data-task-id="social-linkshell" data-task-phase="weekend" data-task-required="false"></span> **Join a social linkshell.** Say that you are a returning player building COR support and looking for weekend learning groups. A patient group is worth more than an extra hour of isolated grinding.

## Readiness check

- [ ] <span data-task-id="weekend-ready" data-task-phase="weekend" data-task-required="true"></span> **Pass this check before volunteering for high-level content.**

- I can name and apply the requested rolls.
- My core equipment is i119 and I have a survival/idle set.
- I have food, ammunition, cards, medicines, and travel access.
- I have read the encounter's current mechanics.
- I tell the leader what I have not unlocked or geared yet.
- I can follow positioning and stop damage to perform support duties.

Some top-end groups require specific weapons, Job Points, Master Levels, or optimized sets. Treat those as requirements for that group and event—not proof that COR cannot contribute anywhere until it is finished.
