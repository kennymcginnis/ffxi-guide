## How to maintain this guide

- Treat official version notes and in-game menus as authoritative when wording or requirements conflict.
- Recheck community camp recommendations after major experience or Trust changes.
- Update the review date when the route is revalidated; do not silently present old monthly advice as current.
- Keep uncertain statements labeled “verify in game” instead of inventing precision.
