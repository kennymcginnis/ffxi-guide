# What Changed Since the 75-cap Era

## The short version

| System | What it means now |
| --- | --- |
| Level cap 99 | Five limit breaks carry a 75 job to 99. |
| Trust | NPC alter egos form a functional solo party for much of progression. |
| Records of Eminence | Objectives award EXP, Sparks, accolades, gear, and tutorial direction during normal play. |
| Rhapsodies of Vana'diel | Key items add cumulative EXP/CP bonuses, Trust capacity, travel savings, and other account-wide acceleration. |
| Home Points / Survival Guides | Travel is dramatically faster once destinations are registered. |
| Item level | At 99, applicable gear raises effective combat power beyond the displayed job level and strengthens summoned Trusts. |
| Ambuscade | Rotating repeatable fights provide an approachable path into useful i119 armor and other rewards. |
| Job Points | Level-99 job progression earned from Capacity Points after obtaining Job Breaker. |
| Master Levels | Long-term progression after mastering a job; every five Master Levels raises the support-job cap by one. |

## The mental-model change

In 2010, reaching the cap and assembling a 75 set were closely connected. Now the path is layered:

**99 → i117 → coherent i119 → job-specific sets → Job Points/mastery → Master Levels**

Do not compare your old equipment directly to endgame screenshots. The first comparison is only against an easily obtained i117 baseline.

## Things not to panic about

- You do not need to finish every expansion story before leveling, but Rhapsodies rewards story progress and should move alongside it.
- You do not need an alliance to earn normal levels; Trusts are the default plan.
- You do not need a finished REMA weapon to begin learning every group activity.
- You do need to read current encounter mechanics and communicate honestly about your gear.
