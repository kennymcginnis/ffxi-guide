## What this baseline implies

- **COR, THF, WAR, RNG, SMN, and BLM** avoid a 1–75 restart.
- **COR** best balances the requested fast first 99 with a useful group identity.
- **THF** is the easiest second practical investment for farming and errands.
- **BRD 66** is close enough to revisit later; its leveling speed is not the real obstacle.
- **GEO and RUN** may be useful eventually, but starting at 0 makes either a slower return route.

Do not assume the visible SMN gear is the only stored equipment. Audit wardrobes, Porter Moogle slips, storage, and delivery boxes before buying replacements.
