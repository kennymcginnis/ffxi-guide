# Phase 1 — COR 75–99

## Fast-path loop

1. Set RoE objectives for the zone and enemy family.
2. Use an EXP ring and food when convenient.
3. Summon a balanced Trust party.
4. Fight enemies that die quickly and still grant good experience; move when chains slow down.
5. Stop just before each cap, complete the limit quest, refresh gear only at the checkpoints below, and continue.
6. Advance Rhapsodies whenever its next key item is close.

**Default solo route:** Trust-led Abyssea is a practical continuation for a returning 75 because alter egos are allowed there. Escha and Grounds/Fields of Valor camps may be faster when access, gear, and target density line up. Camp selection is community-maintained and changes with congestion; use the [BG-Wiki 1–99 guide](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/New_Player_Leveling_Guide) to choose the current camp rather than treating one coordinate as permanent.

If a legitimate player EXP group is already running, join it. Do not wait hours for one—the Trust route is the plan.

## Your COR subjob plan

For this character, use **COR/DNC for the return journey, COR/NIN for safer dual-wielding, and COR/WAR when an organized group wants physical ranged damage**.

| Situation | Subjob | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo leveling, missions, and low-man play | **/DNC** | Healing Waltzes, Spectral Jig, Steps, Haste Samba, and Dual Wield II |
| Safer melee or unfamiliar fights | **/NIN** | Dual Wield III, Utsusemi, Sneak, and Invisible |
| Physical ranged damage in a supported group | **/WAR** | Berserk, Warcry, Aggressor, Double Attack, and Fencer |
| Optimized physical damage later | **/DRG** | Weapon-skill damage traits plus Jump and High Jump; it gains more options through Master Levels |
| Explicit backup-healing assignment | **/WHM** or **/SCH** | Emergency cures and utility when the group specifically asks for them—not a default COR role |

### What to do with your current roster

1. **Raise DNC from 37 to 49 when convenient.** At COR75, `/DNC37` is exactly at the normal half-level cap. It falls behind as COR advances, while `/DNC49` remains fully available at COR99. Follow the tailored [DNC 37–49 detour](dnc-37-49.md) for the subjob, camps, gear, and abilities to practice.
2. **Use /NIN immediately if you do not want the DNC detour yet.** NIN70 already covers the normal level-49 support-job cap and is a strong default for dual-wielding and shadows.
3. **Keep /WAR ready for ranged group damage.** WAR75 already covers the cap. Use it when the party supplies healing and you are fighting primarily at range or using a single-wield/Fencer setup.
4. **Keep /DRG as a later damage option.** DRG66 already qualifies. It is an optimization choice, not something you need to learn before reaching 99.
5. **Do not prioritize /RNG.** Despite the thematic fit and your RNG75, current community COR guidance generally favors `/WAR` for physical ranged damage.

At level 99, the normal support-job cap is 49. Master Levels can eventually raise it as high as 59, so some later guides describe abilities that are unavailable to a freshly leveled COR99. For the route on this page, plan around level-49 support jobs.

These are role-based defaults, not immutable rules. If an encounter leader requests a specific support job, ask what mechanic it covers before changing your setup. See the [community COR support-job guide](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/User%3AArkevorkhat/Piracy_for_Bilgerats%3A_A_Corsair%27s_Guide) and [support-job rules](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Support_Job) for the underlying traits and level limits.

## Gear checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Do this | Skip this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Return / 75 | Current weapon, gun, ammunition, cards, food, and functional armor | Farming old 75-era best-in-slot |
| 75–80 | Use Sparks/NPC/AH upgrades when a key slot is far behind | Replacing every slot for a five-level window |
| 80–90 | Refresh weapon/ammo and obvious accuracy/survival gaps | Expensive rare drops with no 99 use |
| 90–98 | Spend minimally; save Sparks and inventory space for 99 | Building a complete level-90 or 95 set |
| First minute at 99 | Buy/claim an i117 weapon, gun, ammo option, and wearable baseline | Starting JP in old equipment |
| i117 → i119 | Replace the whole baseline through tutorial objectives and Ambuscade | Chasing premium COR weapons before the support floor works |

### COR priorities while leveling

1. Keep ammunition and cards stocked.
2. Learn the dice for the rolls your Trust party benefits from.
3. Use two sensible rolls, stay in range of the intended recipients, then contribute safe damage.
4. Keep melee, marksmanship, and relevant skills moving, but do not halt the EXP route solely to cap every skill.

Exact camp and limit-break requirements above are **community guidance reviewed October 3, 2026**. Verify them in game or on the linked BG-Wiki pages before spending rare currency.
