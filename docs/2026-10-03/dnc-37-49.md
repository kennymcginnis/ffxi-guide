# Support-job Detour — DNC 37–49

> This is a short COR support-job project, not a second main-job grind. Take DNC from 37 to 49, practice the tools COR will inherit, then return to COR.

## The recommendation

Use **DNC/WAR** for the fastest straightforward killing. Your WAR is already 75, and Berserk is available throughout this stretch because it is learned at WAR15. It raises physical attack while lowering defense, so let a tank Trust hold attention and cancel it if incoming damage becomes uncomfortable.

Use **DNC/THF** instead when you value drops or want a positioning exercise. Your THF is already 75, and both of its useful tools arrive within the support-job cap for DNC37–49:

- **Sneak Attack:** stand behind the target while a tank Trust holds its attention.
- **Treasure Hunter I:** a small bonus while collecting crystals, seals, and ordinary drops.

The support job will be capped between level 18 and 24 during this route, so several familiar alternatives are missing their defining abilities:

| Support job | Use it when | Important limitation at DNC37–49 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **/WAR** | Default for fastest kills with a Trust tank and healer | Berserk lowers defense; Double Attack requires WAR25/DNC50 and is just outside this route |
| **/THF** | You want Treasure Hunter or Sneak Attack practice | Sneak Attack requires positioning behind the target |
| **/SAM** | You prefer passive Store TP and Third Eye | It provides no direct attack boost or Dual Wield improvement for this dagger route |
| **/NIN** | You want Utsusemi for extra safety | DNC already has Dual Wield I and gains Dual Wield II at 40, so `/NIN` does not improve its Dual Wield tier here |

Trusts do most of the party-building work, so the choice will not make or break these twelve levels. Use `/WAR` for speed, `/THF` for its small loot benefit, or `/NIN` if a camp feels dangerous.

## Before leaving town

1. Equip two sensible, level-appropriate daggers. Use Sparks, an NPC, the Auction House, or pieces already in storage; do not farm a rare weapon for this detour.
2. Favor accuracy first, then easy DEX, attack, and haste upgrades. Replace only obviously obsolete armor.
3. Activate Records of Eminence objectives for the zone and enemy families.
4. Use an EXP ring and inexpensive accuracy or attack food when convenient.
5. Summon a tank, healer, support Trust, and one or more damage dealers. Use every Trust slot currently unlocked through Rhapsodies.

## Fast 37 → 49 route

| Levels | Default camp | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **37–42** | **Sanctuary of Zi'Tah** | Arrive through the Survival Guide and kill nearby targets that fall quickly. |
| **42–47** | **Labyrinth of Onzozo** | Work near the entrance or Survival Guide on Goblins and Leeches; move when chains slow. |
| **47–49** | **Gustav Tunnel** | Stay near the entrance and favor safe, fast targets over the highest possible check. |

**Alternative at 45+:** use the entrance area of Escha Zi'Tah if Rhapsodies access is ready and the nearby targets are comfortable. Eldieme Necropolis is another community-listed option, but undead sound and low-HP aggro make it less forgiving than this detour needs to be.

Camp levels are community guidance, not guarantees. Trust lineup, Rhapsodies bonuses, EXP campaigns, and congestion all change the pace. Leave a camp when kills become slow or awkward rather than forcing the printed level range. Check the current [BG-Wiki 1–99 leveling guide](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/New_Player_Leveling_Guide) before traveling.

## Practice while leveling

- Use **Quickstep** when accuracy is the problem; use **Box Step** when the party is already connecting reliably.
- Use **Curing Waltz II** for spot healing and **Healing Waltz** to remove an eligible status effect.
- With `/WAR`, use Berserk while a tank Trust holds attention; with `/THF`, try Sneak Attack from behind the target.
- At **DNC40**, add Stutter Step and Reverse Flourish to the rotation; native Dual Wield also improves to tier II.
- At **DNC45**, start using Haste Samba, Curing Waltz III, and Violent Flourish.

The goal is familiarity, not a perfect rotation. COR/DNC mainly values the self-healing, status removal, travel utility, Steps, Samba, and Dual Wield available from the support job. See the [Dancer ability table](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Dancer) for exact level requirements.

## Stop condition

Stop at **DNC49**. That fills the normal support-job cap for COR99. Master Levels can raise support-job limits later, but that is not part of the return path.

Return to [COR 75–99](cor-75-99.md), set `/DNC`, refresh your Trust party, and continue the main route. If you would rather postpone this detour, your existing NIN70 already makes `/NIN` immediately usable through COR99.

For DNC as a future level-99 main job, `/WAR` is the usual general damage option; `/NIN` and `/RUN` are defensive choices, while `/THF` and `/SAM` are encounter-dependent. Those endgame recommendations should not be projected backward onto this brief level-37 route. See the [Community Dancer Guide](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Community_Dancer_Guide) when DNC becomes a serious main-job project.
