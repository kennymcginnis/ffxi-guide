# Phase 4 — Choose the Next Job

Do this after COR is 99 with a usable i119/support foundation. The question is then “what would I enjoy investing in?” rather than “what can get me invited fastest?”

## The low-friction second investment

- [ ] <span data-task-id="thf-99" data-task-phase="future" data-task-required="false"></span> **Raise THF from 75 to 99 when you want a farming fallback.** THF is already at the old cap, is comfortable for errands, and supports drop-focused farming. It is not the recommended first weekend raid identity.

## Pick one lane

- [ ] <span data-task-id="choose-main" data-task-phase="future" data-task-required="false"></span> **Choose one long-term job after sampling the roles below.** Keep COR maintained as the useful bridge; do not gear five jobs at once.

| If this sounds fun… | Consider | Honest warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buff planning and party orchestration | **BRD** | Leveling with Trusts is fine; serious endgame sets can demand several expensive or time-consuming instruments and swaps. |
| Flexible support plus damage | **COR** | You already started here; high-end damage and roll sets still become expensive. |
| Straightforward solo errands and drops | **THF** | Great utility, but group demand depends heavily on the event and gear. |
| Direct physical damage | **WAR** or **SAM** | Easier role concept, but damage slots compete on gear and output. |
| Healing and encounter awareness | **WHM** | Often useful and your WHM is 66, but mistakes are visible and the role requires preparation. |
| Party magic support | **GEO** | Strong identity; serious progression includes job-specific time gates and equipment investment. |
| Tanking and magic defense | **RUN** | Excellent party tools, but it is a new level-0 job and a poor “fastest first 99” choice for this roster. |

### About BRD

Your instinct was directionally right: BRD remains a recognizable group-support role. What changed is that Trusts remove most of the old solo-leveling pain, while endgame expectations moved into equipment sets and instrument progression. BRD is a good *chosen* project after COR—not a prerequisite for returning to the game.
