# Phase 0 — Return Setup

The first session is for restoring the modern solo toolkit, not for proving the old character can still grind in 2010 equipment.

## Account and interface

- [ ] <span data-task-id="account-check" data-task-phase="return" data-task-required="true"></span> **Confirm the account and expansions.** Log in, verify the character and registered expansions, empty delivery-box surprises carefully, and take screenshots of macro books and rare storage before reorganizing anything.

Keep rare, quest, sentimental, and augmented items until you understand modern storage. Move obvious consumables and obsolete vendor gear only after checking them.

## Modern solo foundation

- [ ] <span data-task-id="trusts-unlocked" data-task-phase="return" data-task-required="true"></span> **Unlock Trust magic.** Follow the Records of Eminence tutorial objective that sends you to the starting-city Trust quest, then summon the initial alter egos.
- [ ] <span data-task-id="roe-enabled" data-task-phase="return" data-task-required="true"></span> **Enable Records of Eminence.** Complete the tutorial chain and set repeatable combat objectives before normal play so kills produce experience, Sparks, and accolades in the background.
- [ ] <span data-task-id="travel-basics" data-task-phase="return" data-task-required="true"></span> **Register essential modern travel.** Touch Home Points and Survival Guides as you pass them; prioritize the home city, Ru'Lude Gardens, Mhaura/Selbina, Jeuno, and areas required by the next mission or limit break.
- [ ] <span data-task-id="rhapsody-white" data-task-phase="return" data-task-required="true"></span> **Earn Rhapsody in White.** Begin Rhapsodies of Vana'diel and advance until the next prerequisite blocks you. The first key item grants a 30% EXP/limit-point bonus, faster skill gains, and another Trust slot.

## First Trust party

Use a stable five- or six-member shape when available:

| Slot | Purpose | Early example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tank | Holds attention while you relearn | Valaineral or another available tank |
| Healer | Keeps the chain moving | Kupipi, Apururu, or another healer you own |
| Support | Accuracy, haste, or refresh | Any available support alter ego |
| Damage | Finishes targets quickly | Two available damage Trusts |
| You | COR rolls plus safe damage | Start conservatively; relearn range and hate |

Trust availability varies with login campaigns and progress. Use the best roles you actually own rather than waiting for a perfect named lineup.

## Gear audit before fighting

- Keep the old level-75 set long enough to make a comparison; do not delete it in bulk.
- Buy current bullets/cards and a serviceable gun or melee weapon from Sparks, an NPC, or the auction house. A weapon near your current level matters more than polishing every armor slot.
- Favor basic accuracy, ranged accuracy, attack, and survivability. Your rolls still help even while personal damage is modest.
- Carry a Warp option, food, and an EXP ring if one is available. Do not delay the route to farm a rare level-75 piece.

## Before leaving town

- Set the relevant RoE combat and area objectives.
- Read [COR 75–99](cor-75-99.md) and prepare the next limit break before the experience cap stops you.
- Check the current official [campaign calendar](https://www.playonline.com/pcd/topics/ff11us/list.html) for EXP, Trust, or Abyssea bonuses, but treat campaigns as acceleration rather than a requirement.
