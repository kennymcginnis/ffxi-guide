# BRD Succession Plan

> COR gets you into weekend parties sooner. BRD is the destination. Always advance the first unchecked BRD project, even when another job owns the rest of the session.

## 1. Make the two-song BRD functional

- [ ] <span data-task-id="brd-foundation" data-task-phase="brd" data-task-required="true"></span> **Finish BRD's spellbook, skills, and basic macros.** Confirm the commonly requested March, Minuet, Madrigal, Ballad, Carol, Scherzo, Lullaby, Elegy, and Finale songs; raise Singing, Wind, and String skill; and make working Pianissimo, Tenuto, and song macros.

Use an accessible `All Songs +` instrument such as **Eminent Flute** or **Linos** for real songs while building the better instruments. Practise choosing songs for the party rather than using one fixed pair everywhere.

## 2. Unlock the third song

- [ ] <span data-task-id="brd-three-song" data-task-phase="brd" data-task-required="true"></span> **Obtain and learn a three-song instrument.** Target **Terpander**, Oboro's BRD job-specific instrument, or redeem a Kupon W-Job if one is available. A crafted **Blurred Harp** is an Su2 alternative after 100 BRD Job Points.

For the normal Oboro route, first satisfy one of his qualifying battle clears, then gather **Ebony Harp**, **Shofar**, **Cehuetzi Pelt**, and 150 of a single type: **Pluton**, **Beitetsu**, or **Riftborn Boulder**. The base Terpander already grants the extra song; its later augment is useful but does not block this milestone.

The extra-song instrument creates the slot; it is not automatically the best instrument for the final song's potency. Practise the dummy-song and instrument-swapping sequence until three intended songs can be applied and refreshed without erasing the wrong one. See the [song-slot mechanics](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Category%3AString_instrument) and [Terpander](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Terpander) before choosing the acquisition route.

## 3. Build the support floor

- [ ] <span data-task-id="brd-support-sets" data-task-phase="brd" data-task-required="true"></span> **Build four small BRD sets:** song precast, buff potency/duration, debuff accuracy, and idle/survival. Upgrade pieces because they strengthen one of those sets, not because they share a set name.

Early priorities:

1. `All Songs +` and song-specific potency for the buffs people actually request.
2. Song duration so rotations leave time for mechanics and recovery.
3. Song casting time/recast reduction so the party is not waiting on swaps.
4. Magic accuracy and skill for Lullaby, Elegy, and Finale.
5. Damage taken and magic evasion for the time between casts.

Reforged artifact, relic, and Empyrean pieces often remain useful because of their song-specific bonuses. Check the exact piece before upgrading an entire set.

Use the [BRD Gear & GearSwap checklist](../2026-10-27/brd-gear-checklist.md) for the owned-item tracker, acquisition order, set roles, and downloadable Lua scaffold.

## 4. Practise the real party loop

- [ ] <span data-task-id="brd-party-practice" data-task-phase="brd" data-task-required="true"></span> **Run the full support loop without losing song slots.** Apply three requested songs, split songs with Pianissimo, preserve your own songs with Tenuto when useful, sleep adds, remove dangerous buffs with Finale, and refresh before songs collapse during a mechanic.

Practise first with Trusts or friends where a mistake is recoverable. The goal is calm execution and correct targeting, not a perfect parser result.

## 5. Replace COR for appropriate groups

- [ ] <span data-task-id="brd-weekend-ready" data-task-phase="brd" data-task-required="true"></span> **Pass the BRD handoff check.** Start offering BRD instead of COR for approachable weekend groups that accept the kit below.

- I can reliably maintain three requested songs and explain that I am currently a three-song BRD.
- I have functional precast, potency/duration, debuff-accuracy, and idle/survival sets.
- I can use Pianissimo, Tenuto, Lullaby, Elegy, Finale, Nightingale, Troubadour, Soul Voice, and Clarion Call deliberately.
- I can recover the rotation after a death, dispel, missed target, or song overwrite.
- I have food, medicines, macros, and encounter notes ready.
- I asked the organizer whether the event requires four songs, Honor March, Gjallarhorn-level potency, or another specific instrument.

Keep COR ready when a group needs rolls or has stricter BRD requirements. The handoff is content-specific, not an irreversible job switch.

## Long-term BRD projects

Once the handoff check is real, choose one large instrument project at a time:

- **Daurdabla:** the direct route toward a four-song rotation at the appropriate upgrade stage.
- **Gjallarhorn:** broad song-potency improvement.
- **Marsyas:** access to Honor March through the Aeonic path.
- **Carnwenhan:** major song-duration improvement.

These instruments make BRD stronger and expand which groups will accept it. They are not reasons to postpone practising the three-song job you can build now.

Plan reviewed **October 19, 2026**. Group expectations vary by server, event, and organizer; verify the requested songs and instrument requirements before joining.
