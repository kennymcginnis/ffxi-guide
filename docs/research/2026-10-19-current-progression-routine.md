# Current progression routine — 19 October 2026

**Character scope.** THF, COR, WAR, BLU, RDM, SCH, and BRD are 99 in starter i119; THF and BLU have Su1; COR has some Job Points; all expansion mission lines are complete.  The useful division of labor is **BLU for solo progression and spell collecting, THF for farming, and COR for weekend groups**.  This is deliberately a *small*, repeatable plan—not a list of every capped activity.

## Do these next, in order

1. **Make BLU the first fighting kit.** Keep spell hunting as the reason to play BLU, while replacing starter pieces only when a piece has a job (physical TP/WS, magic, or damage-taken) rather than chasing a matching set.  Use BLU with trusts for the first, forgiving Ambuscade and Odyssey learning runs.  This turns a current hobby into gear and familiarity instead of splitting time across seven jobs.

2. **Build the minimum useful COR support kit before trying to become a damage COR.** The first concrete purchase is **Compensator** from Zurim in Norg: it costs 200 Domain Points and gives `Phantom Roll` duration +20, Snapshot +10, and Triple Shot +10%.  Pair it with an inexpensive/obtainable Barataria Ring, then work toward Chasseur's Gants and a Camulus's Mantle as resources allow.  A current community COR guide puts this *roll set* ahead of ranged TP, weapon-skill, and melee sets; that ordering is sensible for a player whose group role is support. [Domain reward listing (BG-Wiki)](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Domain_Invasion_Rewards) · [COR gearing order (community guide)](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/User%3AArkevorkhat/Piracy_for_Bilgerats%3A_A_Corsair%27s_Guide)

   In parallel, make a simple COR roll macro set and practise choosing two rolls for a melee, ranged, or magic party—especially Chaos/Samurai for conventional melee groups and Wizard's/Warlock's for magic groups.  Clear callouts, correct rolls, and not Busting are more valuable to an entry-level weekend group than an unfinished DD set.

3. **Get COR to the 50-JP gift, then stop treating Job Points as a deadline.** Su1 is at **50** spent JP (not 100); Su2/Su3/Su4 are 100/500/1,200, and Job Master/Su5 is 2,100.  Put the next CP sessions on COR until Su1 if it is not there already; then let COR JP accrue while doing useful content.  BLU and THF are already past that early threshold, so their immediate gains are gear, spells, and farming efficiency—not a forced Master grind. [Official Gifts thresholds (Square Enix)](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/45366-Gifts/threads/45366-Gifts?mode=threaded&p=549840)

4. **Use solo Ambuscade as the bridge from starter i119.** Complete the prerequisite RoE once, then run the current Volume 2 on a difficulty BLU can finish smoothly with trusts (Very Easy is a valid start).  Pick one affordable armor/weapon upgrade target at a time; do not divide Hallmarks among seven jobs.  Ambuscade opponents, prerequisite items, rewards, totals, and currencies rotate at the monthly version update, so spend this month's Hallmarks/Gallantry before the next update rather than banking them. [Ambuscade system/reset behavior (Square Enix)](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/50256-Apr.-5-2016-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=575053&viewfull=1) · [October 2026 rotation update (Square Enix)](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/64770-October-Version-Update?mode=linear&p=677902)

5. **Treat Domain Invasion as a short, targeted route to Compensator—not a compulsory daily.** The normal personal daily cap is **80 Domain Points** and rises to 100 while the server has at least five consecutive Mireu victories; that streak can reset. Regular-invasion awards also scale with the streak, while Mireu's own award is outside the daily maximum. Check the live cap in game and budget roughly two to three or more convenient days for the 200-point purchase. Zurim's catalogue also has selected accessories, Escha items, weapons, and armor, so choose the next item *before* spending points. Join when it is convenient and stop once the Compensator goal is met; latency or a missed window is not lost progression. [Domain Invasion revision (Square Enix)](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/56243-December-10-2019-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=622467&viewfull=1) · [cap and Mireu adjustment (Square Enix)](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/56575-March-10-2020-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=625241&viewfull=1) · [Zurim's current catalogue (BG-Wiki)](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Domain_Invasion_Rewards)

## Low-pressure cadence

### Each play session (pick one anchor, 30–90 minutes)

- **BLU:** farm a named spell target, or run a comfortable Ambuscade Volume 2.
- **THF:** farm a defined currency/material target; do not farm merely because THF is available.
- **COR:** take a short CP session only until the next useful gift, and keep refining the roll macros between fights.
- Activate relevant RoE objectives before leaving town.  RoE permits 30 selected objectives; its limited-time objectives are automatically active and rotate every four hours.  Choose objectives that accompany the anchor, rather than chasing every Copper Voucher. [RoE reference (BG-Wiki)](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/RoE)

### Daily / whenever convenient

- **Until 7 November (distribution) / 9 November (exchange):** log in only if the October Login Campaign rewards are worth the minute to you.  It is live now; **Cipher: Koru-Moru** is a five-point option and is a high-value solo trust if missing.  Spend points before the exchange window ends.  The concurrent Alter Ego Expo boosts Trust HP/MP by 50% and their status resistance through 9 November, making it a pleasant time for BLU spell work—not an obligation. [October 2026 Login Campaign (Square Enix)](https://www.playonline.com/ff11us/campaign/login/login160.html) · [October campaigns (Square Enix)](https://www.playonline.com/pcd2/topics/ff11us/detail/40445/detail.html)
- If a Domain Invasion is already forming and the Compensator target remains, join it. Work toward the live 80–100 cap only when it is quick and enjoyable—this is the only recommended daily-style combat chore.
- Do not force Sortie, Odyssey, Sparks, or Accolades into a daily checklist.

### Once a week

- Schedule **one Ambuscade block**: solo V2 with BLU, or a small weekend group.  Claim the Ambuscade weekly RoE seal if it happens naturally; it also resets after maintenance. [Ambuscade RoE listing (BG-Wiki)](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/RoE)
- Spend Sparks or Unity Accolades only for a chosen purchase or gil need.  Each has a **100,000 weekly exchange limit**; use the in-game currency counter/server reset display for the live reset rather than following an old timezone guide.  It is a selling throttle, not a reason to farm 200,000 currency every week. [July 2020 limit change (Square Enix)](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/56924?p=628113&viewfull=1)
- If a weekend group may run Odyssey, pick up and **hold one Moglophone** in advance.  It is one per character, refreshes 20 hours after receipt, and every entrant consumes one.  There is no stockpile beyond that one held phone.  Begin with Sheol A–C exploration/exit RoEs, not Sheol: Gaol. [Odyssey entry and rewards (Square Enix)](https://we-are-vanadiel.finalfantasyxi.com/post/?id=721&lang=en)

### Once a month

- Check the new version update, then spend Ambuscade currency before it changes.  One modest Ambuscade target per month is enough.
- Complete the easy **Monthly RoE** objectives that fit normal play and claim their Deeds of Heroism.  The four monthly objectives grant 10 Deeds apiece (up to 40 per update cycle), and Deeds are neither reset nor consumed by claiming a reward.  October's operational Group A list is Mog Garden items, one Coalition assignment, 2,000 Mweya Plasm, and a high-tier mission battlefield. Start with Garden/Coalition, assess the Plasm objective separately because consuming Yggrete shards or beads may satisfy it without a Delve clear, and defer only the high-tier battlefield until the BLU/trust kit is comfortable. Inspect the A.M.A.N. Validator's reward tiers before redeeming Deeds—save toward a specific reward rather than selecting a tempting short-term item. [Deeds system (Square Enix)](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/55263-April.-3-2019-%28JST%29-Version-Update?p=614753&viewfull=1) · [current operational rotation (BG-Wiki)](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/RoE)

## Bank access, not anxiety: Odyssey and Sortie

| Content | What can be banked | Practical rule now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Odyssey A–C** | One Moglophone; new one is available 20 hours after receiving it. | Hold one for a planned social run.  Learn A–C and exits with BLU/THF before considering Gaol. |
| **Sheol: Gaol** | Three Moglophone II are required for every entrant; the initial set is granted on first access, then later phones cost 3,000 earned Moogle Segments each. One is consumed per boss attempt and unused phones remain. | Do not grind or spend segments for it yet; Gaol is the later group step after the COR roll set and a reliable i119 fighting kit. |
| **Sortie** | A ready Shiny Ra'Kaznarian Plate can coexist with up to 72,000 seconds of extra credit in Ruspix's Plate. Excess time accrues at one-fifth speed after the ordinary recharge, allowing two consecutive entries. | Do the access/first exploratory run when ready, then bank at most that one extra recharge for a specific Empyrean or guided group objective. It is not a multi-day key stockpile. |

Square Enix confirms that Sortie yields gallimaufry for Empyrean upgrades/Prime weapons, but that is a later payoff, not a requirement to start improving the current kit. The same official guide explains the normal 20-hour Sortie recharge, Ruspix's extra stored recharge, and Gaol's three-Moglophone-II requirement. [Square Enix returning-adventurer guide](https://we-are-vanadiel.finalfantasyxi.com/post/?id=721&lang=en)

## What to defer without regret

- Hard Ambuscade, Omen/Dynamis-D, Sortie boss routes, Sheol: Gaol, Master Levels, and Prime weapons.
- A full COR ranged damage suite or a second/third endgame job.  First be the COR who can maintain the right rolls with a duration set.
- Capping Domain Points, Sparks, Unity Accolades, daily RoE, and every monthly Deed.  Missed caps are not lost character power; a completed Compensator, one useful Ambuscade upgrade, a new BLU spell, or a reliable COR macro is.
