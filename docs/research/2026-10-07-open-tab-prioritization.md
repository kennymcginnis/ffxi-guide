# Open-tab triage for a mostly-solo 99: October 7, 2026

## Bottom line

Put the account, not another mid-level job, first. The active jobs are **THF 99, COR 99, and BLU 99**; use whichever currently has the strongest item-level weapon and armor to build an i119 baseline, unlock reliable Trust/travel infrastructure, and begin easy Ambuscade. Keep **COR** as the weekend-group gearing target. BRD 96/SCH 92/PUP 82 are reasonable later projects, but the 75-and-below jobs do not determine the next solo or weekend-group milestone.

**Immediate sequence:** start Seekers and Imprimatur accrual → establish the Fresh 99 item-level floor → unlock Adoulin travel and complete *I'm on a Boat* while Imprimaturs regenerate → finish Seekers 1-6 and 1-7 → resume Rhapsodies 3-4 → begin low-difficulty Ambuscade. Set up Unity and Koru-Moru in parallel; touch Survival Guides as you encounter them.

## The prerequisite question, resolved

These three tabs are **not one straight three-page chain**:

```text
Seekers 1-5 ──> start Imprimatur timer
       │
       ├──> I'm on a Boat (fame helper)
       └──> Seekers 1-6 ──> Seekers 1-7 ──> Rhapsodies 3-4

Dancers in Distress ──> later Wings of the Goddess story
                       (it follows, rather than unlocks, the RoV checkpoint)
```

Rhapsodies 3-4 explicitly needs Seekers 1-7, not merely 1-6. Seekers 1-6 needs Imprimaturs spent; talking to a Task Delegator after 1-5 starts that real-time accrual. *I'm on a Boat* helps meet the fame condition, but it is not a hard prerequisite. *Dancers in Distress* is a Wings of the Goddess mission that follows *In the Name of the Father*—the latter is the RoV checkpoint—so the supplied Dancers tab is not itself a RoV prerequisite. [RoV 3-4](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Rhapsodies_of_Vanadiel_Mission_3-4), [Seekers 1-6](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Seekers_of_Adoulin_Mission_1-6), [Seekers 1-7](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Seekers_of_Adoulin_Mission_1-7), [RoV dependency list](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Rhapsodies_of_Vanadiel_Missions), [official RoV key-item benefits](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxi/threads/46977).

## What is actionable this weekend

1. On whichever **level-99 job has the best current item-level equipment**, finish the basic RoE/Trust chain, join a Unity, and obtain Koru-Moru if missing. Keep COR as the group-support gearing target.
2. Start Seekers through 1-5, speak to a Task Delegator immediately, complete *I'm on a Boat*, and do easy Coalition assignments as Imprimaturs arrive. Begin Adoulin traversal while doing it.
3. Follow the whole Fresh 99 guide—not its already-complete “Achieve Level 99” subsection—until the character has a coherent i119 floor.
4. Run **easy/very-easy Ambuscade** with Trusts for Hallmarks, then make the Kaja Sword/Naegling goal a COR/BLU weapon project. It is not a THF weapon path. Save difficult/Intense Ambuscade and multi-set optimization for the weekend group.
5. Continue Seekers to 1-7, then clear RoV 3-4. Leave *Dancers in Distress* for optional Wings story progression; work the separate *In the Name of the Father* RoV gate when the later RoV mission actually asks for it.
