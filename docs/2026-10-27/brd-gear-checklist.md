# BRD Gear & GearSwap

> Build a reliable three-song support BRD first. The checklist records what you own; it does not add forty new obligations to the command-center queue.

This page turns Funkworkz's 2022 BRD guide into a smaller, support-first acquisition plan. It intentionally leaves melee, cure, tanking, and highly specialized side sets out of the owned-item checklist. The original guide is a useful opinionated reference, not a current best-in-slot contract.

## Start these clocks now

Only two early lanes deserve calendar urgency. Everything else below is either repeatable, purchasable, a long grind, or not realistically solo-friendly yet.

| Clock | Mostly-solo action | What it advances | Urgency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ambuscade month** | Clear the easiest Volume 2 difficulty you can repeat. Claim the current month's rewards before they rotate. | **Intarabus's Cape** and its capped monthly augment materials; **Inyanga Jubbah +2**, **Inyanga Shalwar +2**, and **Ayanmo Cosciales +2** progression | **Do this first.** Cape augment-material purchases are monthly-limited, so an unused month cannot be recovered later. |
| **Sortie entry charge** | Enter on **BRD** at least once, then learn a short upper-floor chest route you can complete alone. Bank Gallimaufry, Ra'Kaznar Sapphires, and earring cases. | **Fili +2** upgrades and the random **Fili Earring** path | **Start now, spend later.** Entry eligibility recharges over 20 hours, and entering on BRD unlocks its five Empyrean upgrade slots at Ruspix. |
| **Monthly campaigns** | Check the official campaign page and use Ambuscade/Sortie bonuses when they actually appear. | Faster progress rather than an exclusive required item | **Opportunistic.** Never wait for a campaign before starting the normal route. |

For Sortie, your first goal is establishing the BRD unlock and banking resources—not buying an 80,000-Gallimaufry Old Case or starting Loughnashade. Upgrade useful Fili pieces to +2 before spending the same clock on Prime-weapon ambitions. See the [official returning-player Sortie guide](https://we-are-vanadiel.finalfantasyxi.com/post/?id=721&lang=en), [Ruspix and the BRD-entry unlock](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Ruspix), [Ra'Kaznar Sapphire](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Ra%27Kaz._Sapphire), and [JSE cape monthly limits](https://www.bg-wiki.com/ffxi/Category:JSE_Capes).

The **artifact/relic +1 pieces, Terpander, Kali, Linos, Moonbow Whistle, defensive accessories, and old REMA grinds are not use-it-or-lose-it clocks**. Farm or buy them when they are the next useful upgrade. Carnwenhan's old Assault/Einherjar waits are also greatly shortened by Rhapsody in Azure, which you already have from finishing Rhapsodies, so it is not an urgent daily chore.

## Your 100-million-gil plan

You already own **Moonbow Whistle +1**, which removes the most obvious expensive crafted BRD purchase. Check its owned-item box below and spend **zero** gil replacing it.

Auction House prices vary by world and can change faster than this guide. Before bidding, check your world's recent sale history on [FFXIAH](https://www.ffxiah.com/) and treat the numbers below as **maximum personal allocations**, not price claims.

| Order | Allocation | Spend it on | Rule |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | **20M reserve** | Food, medicines, reforging fees, unexpected materials, and future opportunities | Do not invest the entire bankroll in one listing. This reserve is part of the plan. |
| 2 | **Up to 5M** | The missing auctionable/bazaar components for **Terpander** | Finish the three-song gate first. Buy only the exact missing Ebony Harp, Shofar, Cehuetzi Pelt, and chosen stack of 150 Plutons, Beitetsu, or Riftborn Boulders after confirming Oboro's requirements. |
| 3 | **Up to 25M** | **One Stikini Ring +1** | This is the first large direct gear purchase: skill, magic accuracy, and Refresh remain useful in debuff and idle sets. If your world's price is above this personal cap, use the NQ or an earned alternative and wait. Do not buy a second +1 yet. |
| 4 | **Up to 10M** | Exact reforge ingredients, Linos augment stones, Echo Drops, Remedies, food, and other small blockers | Buy materials only when the base item, chapters, unlock, and upgrade NPC are ready. Avoid speculative stacks that sit in storage. |
| 5 | **At least 40M remaining** | A dedicated **Gjallarhorn / Daurdabla fund** | Gil converts efficiently into Gjallarhorn's relic currency; Daurdabla is the song-count project but still requires substantial personal farming and later tradable upgrade materials. Price the entire next stage before buying the first expensive stack. |

That allocation totals 100M only if every purchase reaches its cap. A cheaper purchase leaves more in the instrument fund; it is not permission to find another sidegrade.

### Recommended decision after the first purchases

Run **Terpander + Moonbow Whistle +1 + one Stikini Ring +1** while building the earned armor sets. At that point:

1. Continue farming **Daurdabla toward level 99** because four songs are the larger weekend-group acceptance gate.
2. Use gil to accelerate **Gjallarhorn** when you have priced the complete relic-currency path and can finish it without touching the 20M reserve.
3. Buy Daurdabla's expensive tradable 95/99 materials only after the preceding Empyrean trials are complete; otherwise the gil is trapped in an unfinished project.
4. Do not buy Blurred Harp +1, a second Stikini Ring +1, Su3 Mousai pieces, or DD-BRD luxuries until your real-song rotation, duration set, and survival set are functional.

This makes Gjallarhorn the best **gil-funded** major project while Daurdabla remains the more important **play-funded** succession project. They can advance in parallel without pretending 100M can purchase a finished four-song BRD.

## What each GearSwap set does

| Set | When it equips | What belongs in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sets.precast.FC` | Non-song magic begins | Generic fast cast |
| `sets.precast.Song` | A song begins | Song casting-time reduction; not potency gear |
| `sets.midcast.Song` | A normal song resolves | Skill, duration, and broad song bonuses |
| `sets.midcast.Dummy` | A placeholder song resolves | Terpander or another extra-song instrument; potency is irrelevant |
| `sets.midcast.Ballad`, `.Minuet`, etc. | That song family resolves | Song-specific potency pieces layered over the normal song set |
| `sets.midcast.Lullaby` | Lullaby resolves | Lullaby effect/duration plus enough accuracy |
| `sets.midcast.Lullaby.Resistant` | Manually selected hard target | Maximum practical skill and magic accuracy |
| `sets.midcast.Debuff` | Elegy, Finale, Requiem, Threnody | Skill and magic accuracy |
| `sets.JA.*` | Nightingale, Troubadour, Soul Voice | The matching relic enhancement piece |
| `sets.idle` | Nothing is being cast | Damage reduction, magic evasion, refresh, and movement |

GearSwap does not need a macro for every item. Macros invoke songs and abilities; the Lua equips an entire set for each stage. Download [the starter `brd_gearsets.lua`](../assets/gearswap/brd_gearsets.lua), copy it to Windower's `GearSwap/data/<character>/` folder, rename it `BRD.lua`, and replace entries with gear you actually own.

Useful console commands:

```text
//gs reload
//gs export
//gs c toggle resist
```

Use `//gs export` while wearing augmented gear, then copy the generated exact item table into the Lua. If a listed item is absent from your inventory, GearSwap cannot equip it; edit the set rather than trusting a silent partial swap. Test the file on harmless songs before a party.

## Rotation notes from the emailed guide

- **Merits:** the guide favors Minne/Minuet in Group 1 and 5/5 Nightingale plus 5/5 Troubadour in Group 2. Treat that as a support-first recommendation, not an irreversible law.
- **Job Points:** its proposed order starts with Minuet effect, song accuracy, Requiem effect, Lullaby duration, Marcato, and Clarion Call before the remaining categories. Your immediate gate is still functional equipment and practice, not a perfect JP order.
- **Support jobs:** `/WHM` is the general healing/support default; `/RDM` adds fast cast, Phalanx, and utility; `/DNC` or `/NIN` support dual-wield/melee situations; `/PLD` is a niche pulling/tanking choice.
- **Skill practice:** keep Singing, Wind, and String current. The guide suggests Ionis and Uragnites around Ceizak/Yahse for repeated low-risk Threnody casts; verify the camp is safe and appropriate before using it.
- **Third and fourth songs:** use short, harmless dummy songs with the extra-song instrument to create slots, then cast the intended real songs in potency/duration gear. Refresh deliberately so an old real song is not mistaken for a dummy and overwritten.
- **Tools:** the email recommends GearSwap plus visibility helpers such as Timers, Distance/DParty, PartyBuffs, and color-coded song icons. Add only the tools that reduce mistakes for you.
